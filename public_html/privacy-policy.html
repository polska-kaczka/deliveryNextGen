2. Data we process

2.1 Contact and demo request form

First and last name, business email address, company name, optional phone number, type of business, plan of interest, the content of your message, and the confirmations you tick in the form. Please do not send special categories of data (e.g. health data) in the message.

2.2 Correspondence

If you write to us by email or call us, we process your contact details and the content of the correspondence.

2.3 Technical data

  • Server logs kept by our hosting provider: IP address, date and time, requested URL, browser type (user agent), HTTP status.
  • Anti-abuse data: when you submit the form, a one-way hashed (salted) form of your IP address is stored for up to 1 hour to limit repeated submissions.
  • Session cookie set only when you start using the form, to protect it against forged requests (CSRF) — see the Cookie policy.

We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking on this website.

4. Retention

  • Form enquiries and correspondence: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless a contract is concluded — then for the term of the contract and the limitation period for claims.
  • Anti-abuse IP hash: up to 1 hour.
  • Session cookie: until you close your browser (or after 30 minutes of inactivity on the server).
  • Server logs: according to the hosting provider's retention period, typically no longer than 90 days.
  • Data needed for legal claims: until the claims become time-barred.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy (Art. 15);
  • rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • erasure (“right to be forgotten”) (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • data portability, where processing is based on a contract and automated (Art. 20);
  • object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal (Art. 7(3)) — currently we do not rely on consent on this website.

To exercise your rights, email danielmedinabello5@gmail.com. We respond without undue delay, at the latest within one month (extendable by two months in complex cases, of which we will inform you). We may ask for information needed to confirm your identity.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement, for example:

  • PLPresident of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl;

9. Voluntary provision

Providing data is voluntary. Without the fields marked as required we cannot answer your enquiry. Optional fields (phone number, plan of interest) help us prepare but are not needed.

10. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). The anti-spam checks only block repeated or automated submissions.

11. Cookies and local storage

This website uses only strictly necessary cookies and local storage. Details, including how to manage them, are in the Cookie policy.

12. Security

We protect data with appropriate technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR), including encrypted HTTPS connections, a strict Content Security Policy, protection of the form against forged requests and automated abuse, limited access to mailboxes, and data minimisation — we collect only what we need to answer you.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when our processing or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page with the date of the last update.
