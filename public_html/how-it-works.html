How it works

From order to doorstep. Automatically.

Our platform handles everything between “order placed” and “delivered”. Your team keeps cooking, packing and selling.

Four steps

What happens during a delivery.

Every trip follows the same four steps — whether it's dinner, a parcel or lab samples.

  1. Order placed

    An order arrives from your app, online shop, POS or our API, with pickup point, address and time window.

  2. Dispatched

    The nearest robot with enough charge is assigned and the route is planned for current conditions.

  3. On the way

    The robot drives on pavements and pedestrian areas, avoiding obstacles. Everyone can follow it live.

  4. Handed over

    A one-time PIN opens the compartment. Proof of delivery is logged and the robot heads to its next job.

Safety and compliance

Designed to share the street responsibly.

A person in the loop

If a robot can't resolve a situation itself, an operator is alerted and can take remote control within seconds.

Permits handled

Rules for delivery robots differ by city. We handle permits and confirm coverage before you sign.

Privacy by design

Navigation data is used to drive safely, not to profile people. Details are agreed in each deployment's data processing terms.
