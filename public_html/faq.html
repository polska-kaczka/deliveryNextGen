Our robots are designed for hard-to-access areas — narrow streets, pedestrian-only zones, gated estates and hilly terrain. The navigation system adapts to changes around it and re-routes automatically if an obstacle appears.

The robot detects obstacles in real time and recalculates its route. If it can't resolve the situation itself, our operations centre is alerted and an operator can take remote control within seconds.
