About us

We build for the last hundred metres.

Millions of deliveries fail every day because they can't reach the door. We started DeliveryNextGen to change that — one neighbourhood at a time.

Our mission

Our mission

Make every address reachable — the alley, the pedestrian zone, the gated estate — without adding another van to streets that were never built for one.

How we launch in a new city

From first call to a running fleet.

Every deployment follows the same five steps, so you know what happens and when before anything goes on the street.

  1. Step 1

    Coverage check

    We map your delivery area, pavements and access points and confirm that robots can operate there.

  2. Step 2

    Local compliance

    We obtain the permits and agreements the city or site owner requires.

  3. Step 3

    Pilot

    A small fleet starts on selected routes. We measure delivery times and reliability together with you.

  4. Step 4

    Integration

    Orders flow automatically from your app, shop or POS into our platform.

  5. Step 5

    Scale

    Based on the pilot data, the fleet grows to match your volume and opening hours.

What we stand for

Safety first

Robots share the pavement with people. Supervision, conservative speeds and remote operators come before efficiency.

Lighter cities

Electric, compact and quiet — less traffic and no tailpipe emissions where people live.

Honest partnership

Clear prices, clear contracts and coverage confirmed before you sign.
