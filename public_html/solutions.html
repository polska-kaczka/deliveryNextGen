Solutions

Built for the places vans can't reach.

Every business has its own last mile. Pick yours to see how a robot fleet fits into your day.

Restaurants

Hot food, on time, every time.

Peak hours shouldn't depend on how many couriers happen to be online. Restaurant mode gives your orders a priority queue and insulated compartments for short delivery windows.

  • Insulated compartment that keeps temperature during the trip
  • Priority dispatch for restaurant orders at peak times
  • Works with your ordering app or POS
  • Customers track the robot live and open it with a PIN
Demo for restaurants

Retail

Same-day delivery from your own shelf.

Turn your shop or micro-warehouse into a local fulfilment hub. Orders placed online leave in minutes, not tomorrow.

  • Plugins for popular shop platforms, plus a REST API
  • Delivery slots your customers choose at checkout
  • PIN hand-over — no more parcels left on the doorstep
  • Delivery status pushed to your customer service tools
Demo for retail

Logistics

Hand over the hardest final drops.

Vans stop at the edge of the old town; robots take it from there. Combine your network with our fleet for the addresses your drivers can't reach efficiently.

  • Transfer points at your hubs or at the zone boundary
  • Status webhooks into your TMS or WMS
  • Proof of delivery logged for every parcel
  • Custom SLAs on the Enterprise plan

Campuses

A delivery network inside your gates.

Universities, business parks, hospitals and residential estates — closed areas with many addresses and controlled access are ideal for a permanent fleet.

  • Fixed stations and routes agreed with site management
  • Internal deliveries: mail, lab samples, catering, retail
  • Access control respected — no gates left open
  • White-label dashboard for the site operator
