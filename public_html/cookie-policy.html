1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. This policy covers both.

We do not use analytics, advertising, profiling or social-media cookies, and we do not embed third-party content (videos, maps, social plugins) that sets cookies.

3. Third parties

Fonts are delivered by Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., Slovenia, EU). When the fonts load, your browser connects to their servers and shares your IP address, but Bunny Fonts does not set cookies. See the Privacy policy for details.

4. Legal basis

Storing or accessing information on your device requires your consent, unless it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested. Both items listed above are strictly necessary, so no consent is required:

  • EUArticle 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive);
  • PLArticle 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej);
  • ESArticle 22(2) of Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the AEPD Guide on the use of cookies.

If we ever introduce optional cookies (for example analytics), they will remain off until you actively switch them on; refusing will be as easy as accepting, and you will be able to withdraw consent at any time.

5. Managing your choices

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie will prevent the contact form from working; you can still contact us by email or phone.

6. Changes

We update this policy whenever we change the technologies used on the website. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
