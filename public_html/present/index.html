Why we exist

Most deliveries don’t fail on the motorway.

They fail in the last hundred metres.

  • Too narrow for a van
  • Pedestrian zone
  • No gate code

Meet the robot

Built for the pavement.

  • 100% electric
  • Works 24/7
  • PIN-locked cargo
  • Supervised live

Inside the robot

Everything the last metres need.

ConnectivityMobile data and satellite positioning for live tracking and remote supervision.

Multi-wheel chassisStable on cobbles, kerb ramps and uneven old-town surfaces.

Insulated compartmentKeeps food warm and parcels dry. Opens only with the recipient's PIN.

Perception sensorsCameras and distance sensors detect pedestrians, kerbs and obstacles.

Electric driveFully electric, recharged at stations between jobs.
