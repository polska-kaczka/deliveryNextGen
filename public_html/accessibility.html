Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, regardless of device, ability or assistive technology.

Last updated: 18.10.2026Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA

1. Commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the reference standard of EN 301 549. This statement is published voluntarily: our services are offered to business customers only.

2. Measures

  • Semantic HTML with landmarks, headings in logical order and a “Skip to content” link;
  • full keyboard operation with visible focus indicators, including the menu, FAQ, cookie settings and form;
  • form fields with labels, required fields marked, and error messages announced to screen readers;
  • text alternatives for meaningful graphics; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology;
  • colour contrast checked for text and controls on the dark theme;
  • content that reflows on small screens and when zoomed to 200%.

3. Motion & animation

The website uses scroll-based animations to tell the story of a delivery. If your device is set to reduce motion (“Reduce motion” on macOS/iOS, “Show animations” off on Windows, “Remove animations” on Android), all animations are disabled and the content is shown immediately. No content flashes more than three times per second.

4. Known limitations

  • The interactive route map on the home page is illustrative; the same information is provided as text in the list of steps next to it.
  • External fonts may not load in some corporate networks; the website then uses system fonts with no loss of content.

5. Feedback

If you find a barrier or need information in another format, write to danielmedinabello5@gmail.com. We reply within 14 days.
