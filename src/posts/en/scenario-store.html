Starting point

An online store with a micro-warehouse in the city. Around 250 orders a month within a few kilometres, mostly small parcels. Customers ask for same-day delivery, but couriers collect only once a day.

Choosing a plan

The Starter plan is enough to begin: 1 robot, up to 300 deliveries a month, GPS tracking, PIN-secured cargo and API access. At €299 net and 250 deliveries, that’s about €1.20 net per delivery. If volume exceeds the limit we’ll suggest the next plan — nothing is charged without your written agreement.

Integration in two steps

  1. Week 1: CSV import from your order export. No coding, first deliveries straight away.
  2. Later: REST API and webhooks. The store creates a delivery automatically when an order is paid, and the “delivered” status flows back.

What a day looks like

Orders placed by early afternoon leave the micro-warehouse within minutes of packing. The customer chooses to collect right away, and the robot waits at the address until someone enters the PIN.

What to measure

  • share of orders delivered the same day,
  • failed deliveries compared with the courier,
  • the effect of same-day delivery on checkout conversion.
