@@ notes.2
The problem: the last hundred metres. The van stops at the old town, a pedestrian zone or a gate — that’s where deliveries break.
@@ notes.3
Our answer: a compact electric robot built for the pavement. Name the four labels.
@@ notes.4
Anatomy: connectivity, multi-wheel chassis, insulated compartment, perception sensors, electric drive.
@@ notes.5
One order from 21:47 to 22:03. Point at the obstacle — the robot re-routes by itself. The PIN opens the compartment at the end.
@@ notes.6
Platform: the whole fleet on one screen. Teams learn it in an afternoon. Data is simulated.
@@ notes.7
Integrations: REST API, webhooks, plugins, CSV or manual dispatch. About 48 hours to go live.
@@ notes.8
People behind the robots: if a robot can’t solve a situation, an operator takes over within seconds.
@@ notes.9
The numbers. At −60% say it’s an estimate that depends on volume — see the footnote.
